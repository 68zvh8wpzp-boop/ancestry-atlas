## Existing published source evidence
- Harrison E. Webb, “Samuel Webb of Saybrook, Conn.,” *The American Genealogist* 22(1) (July 1945):44–47, pages **44–47 reviewed completely** in Tranche 128. At p44 he cites Saybrook Land Records II:209, a **31 July 1707 deed by Deacon William Parker to John Webb**, 25 acres at Pataconk. Separate 1731 deed Saybrook IV:340 and associated bond reportedly name Samuel and Lydia as children of deceased John Webb; these are the author's quoted primary-era words, **not an inspected original record**. He infers John Webb the younger was another sibling. See saved T128 Library report.
