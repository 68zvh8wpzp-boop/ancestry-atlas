## Critical interpretive guardrails

- A **will-book index** is *not* a general index to all estates. A person could die **intestate** and leave an administration file/letters, have a will recorded in another jurisdiction, be recorded outside these dates, use a name variant, or be omitted/miscopied in the published transcriptions.
- This does **not** prove that the 1754 candidate never died in Albany County or lacked a will. It does **not** establish that the 1773 Chester James Webb–Elizabeth Douglas marriage was his, or that he fathered the 1777 James Webb of the approved direct Webb line.
- The published 1754 candidate profile itself assigns children Stephen (born 1777), Curtis, Samuel, Elizabeth and Polly; none of those assertions is treated as independent period parentage evidence.
- Church baptism images and State Library church index slips remain behind separate dynamic sign-in restrictions (tranche02); we **did not hit those exact same URLs again** or claim negative baptism results.
- No person/source photo saved; no live Atlas relationship edge or confidence changed; no FamilySearch editing, email use, paid records, or repeat census audit.
