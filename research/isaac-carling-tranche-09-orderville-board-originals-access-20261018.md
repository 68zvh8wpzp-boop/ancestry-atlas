## 2. NEW: Independently digitized primary records within Huntington microfilm

**Institution:** The Huntington Library, San Marino, California, **MSS MFilm 00207**, James Godson Bleak, [`Historical Memoranda of the Southern Mission [microform], approximately 1866–1904`](https://www.huntington.org/collections/lib-p16003coll15-13715).

**Nature and provenance:** Huntington catalogs a **779-image fully digitized microfilm reel** copied from original material loaned by Glenn E. Snow through Juanita Brooks **11 August 1950**. It is NOT the same collection as BYU MSS 512 and must remain a distinct archival provenance. The institution specifically describes, among other southern Utah originals:

- **Orderville United Order Articles of Association;**
- **1879 original financial statements;**
- **lists of people who joined or left during 1875–1876;**
- **names of Orderville board members and directors;**
- history and correspondence for Orderville, including material as late as 1904.

**This is a materially stronger access path than a mere physical-record catalog** because its item page exposes a viewer with 779 individual image frames. However, none of these 779 frames is indexed by document type, person name, date or internal page range in the public listing, and the web-text interface shows **image-number navigation but no OCR/transcription**. Clicking the frame thumbnails led to a Huntington dynamically served IIIF thumbnail that could not be read as a manuscript image with the available extraction/viewer connection. The local container failed to resolve `rail.huntington.org`, so no full-resolution IIIF image or complete searchable film was transferred.

**Name-proof limitation:** An institution says its reel has board director rosters; **it does NOT say Isaac appears in them**. No specific frame/folio bearing `Isaac Carling` was visually read, and no claim of first vice president, signatures, original job title or complete transcription can be based on this data. Do **not** label the Huntington reel `Isaac's board-minutes original` until a named entry is found.

**Distinct cross-custodian original-copy lead:** Utah State Historical Society, `James Godson Bleak Papers`, finding aid https://history.utah.gov/finding-aids/data/B00171/b0171.html, **Box 2, Folders 4–5**, description `Orderville (Utah) United Order`. That is a physical 1988 courtesy copy of papers also held/derived from other repositories, NOT automatically the original 19th-century manuscript and NOT a proved name-bearing entry. This narrows a possible alternate reproduction route.

**Why not manually inspect 779 frames:** With no subject index/film division locations and a viewer not exposing full manuscript pixels here, indiscriminate sequential inspection would create a large open-ended duplication loop and poor evidence quality. A Work browser with actual page viewing can navigate the Huntington viewer or a curator can identify reel section/frames; this chat cannot claim completed frame-level inspection.

## 3. Isaac's later family-history assertions, and one false-positive correction

Elda P. Mortenson-compiled Carling history [later family reproduction](https://de.scribd.com/document/48194950/Isaac-Van-Wagoner-Carling) states Isaac joined the Orderville Ward bishopric as **first counselor on 5 August 1877**, served until around **1883**, was **first vice president of the United Order**, foreman of **carpentry**, operated **painting**, and helped sons build a **co-op store**. This is a coherent narrative that already existed before this tranche, but **not a direct transcription of a particular MSS 512 dated board entry**. Each job and precise date remains a source-level **secondary assertion**. Avoid conflating a church bishopric role with an elected economic co-op director.

One tempting BYU item **`The story of Isaac's new trousers`, MSS SC 1610** is a **1935 fictionalized biography about Isaac E. Black**, according to its own archival person authority and scope, **not** Isaac Van Wagoner Carling. [Institutional finding aid](https://archives.lib.byu.edu/repositories/ltpsc/resources/upb_msssc1610). **Reject as unrelated.** Do not accidentally import its prose, photos, trades or local history into Isaac Carling's personal page.
