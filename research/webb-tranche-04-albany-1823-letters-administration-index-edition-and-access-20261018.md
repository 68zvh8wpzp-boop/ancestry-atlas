## Grounded new source: printed official-court index published 1895

*Index to wills and to letters of administration: Index to wills from 1780 to December 1, 1895; Index to letters of administration from 1794 to December 1, 1895*, prepared under direction of **Martin D. Conway** for Albany County Surrogate's Court, Albany: **J. B. Lyon, 1895**, approximately **240 printed pages**. Documented as a separate historical Albany County publication by the **New York Public Library bibliography**:
https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/0c/County_government%2C_including_county_publications%3B_references_to_material_in_the_New_York_Public_Library_%28IA_countygovernment00newyrich%29.pdf

Independent catalog:
https://search.worldcat.org/title/16402895 (record labels it 1895 microform and attributes court/Conway);
https://en.geneanet.org/library/doc/5458557/index-to-wills-and-to-letters-of-administration-index-to-wills-from-1780-to-dec-1-1895 (Geneanet digitized edition, **482 scan images**, *not* a 482-print-page book; the 240-page printed volume may have spread pages digitized separately);
https://www.werelate.org/wiki/Source:Albany,_New_York,_United_States._Index_to_Wills_and_to_Letters_of_Administration (bibliographic entry gives FamilySearch microfilm identifier **6062100**, *not yet verified directly with FS catalog*).

**Critical result:** This consolidated book exists and its administration part spans 1823. The **Webb alphabetical entry/page was NOT inspected**. Geneanet explicitly says reading full book requires **Premium**, a paid tier; user permits free sources only. The WebReader cannot see the surname page without an authorized session. A constructed Geneanet filtered-WEBB catalog URL also proved inaccessible; don't claim Geneanet's OCR name matches or absence as an index search. The separate WorldCat page requires browser JavaScript, but its bibliographic card is visible in search results. The historic NYPL county bibliography supplies the printed-author/publication details, rather than assuming the title itself proves James has an estate.

## Explicit elimination of wrong *administration* leads

**Free online publisher New Horizons Genealogy, Albany Wills/Probate hub**:
https://mail.newhorizonsgenealogicalservices.com/ny-genealogy/albany-county/albany_county_new_york_wills_probate_records.htm

This offers exactly TWO separate `Letters of Administration` volumes online:
- Volume 1 **1794–1805**;
- Volume 2 **1805–1810**.

Neither volume covers the competing man's **1823** alleged death. **They have NOT been searched as a false negative** and must not be used to claim “no James Webb administration.” This absence of a relevant *online coverage period* is substantive governance, preventing future back-to-back unproductive searches.

**Albany County official Hall of Records** describes a separate **Surrogate Court alphabetic 1787–1845 index to wills and letters of administration**, and an **Index to Wills and Letters 1780–1895**, with original custody; it is not a verified Webb entry:
https://www.albanycountyny.gov/world-our-fathers-made-albany-county-records-in-the-constitutional-era-1783-1815?contentId=353ce58b-6247-46f7-80a3-5ed13c0667bc

The **New York Genealogical & Biographical Society** separately describes the Albany Surrogate's **all-records index 1787–1959** on microfilm and an early printed book to 1895; its *letters-of-administration microfilm* noted separately only covers 1794–1815, which is too early for 1823. These are different access points/classes:
https://www.newyorkfamilyhistory.org/knowledgebase/new-york-probate-records-1787

**Mislabel warning, independently useful:** New York State **Court of Probates `J0033` 1700–1825 statewide estate papers** are commonly MIS-CATALOGUED as Albany County estate records by some genealogical libraries. This would lead to a false geographic assumption if searching a James Webb only because the papers physically sat in Albany. NYG&B specifically warns of this: https://www.newyorkfamilyhistory.org/content/new-york-probate-records-1787 . Thus do not import an unrelated statewide James Webb estate into Albany's local Surrogate's record without a residence statement.
