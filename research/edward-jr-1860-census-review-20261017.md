### Reconciliation

Two distinct pages list an Edward M., age 13, born Missouri, marked as attending school: page 101 on 12 July with the Owens household, and page 114 on 16 July with Caroline A. McRae and Deloss Webb. Both are attached to Edward Jr. The index surname “Mc Rae” for page 114 is inherited from the household head; Edward’s actual line contains no surname. It is not evidence of a name change. The matching age, birthplace, given name and middle initial, together with the Caroline/Deloss family context, make a possible double enumeration a reasonable interpretation. These pages alone do not prove why it occurred, where he normally resided, or whether he moved between 12 and 16 July. Neither entry is being discarded or labelled an erroneous attachment.
