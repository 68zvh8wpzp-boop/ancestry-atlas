## 2. NEW exact named source reference and as-published content

**Source:** *Welch Family*, a compiled Welch-family records file preserving a **July–September 1993 genealogical publication, page 56**, section headed `OTSEGO CO RECORDS continued.` Hosted by **Orange County California Genealogical Society (OCCGS)**:
https://occgs.com/projects/rescue/family_files/files/WELCH%20Family.pdf

**As indexed by search engine, precise published abstract**, preserving original capitalization and meaningful abbreviated fragments, ***NOT*** a diplomatic transcription of the 1804 deed:

> `K:372  DATED 6/27/1804 between James WEBB of Brookfield, Chenango County (now Madison) and JOHN WELCH of the Town of Otsego, County of Otsego...$63.75....part of Lot #57 in Niels Patent...12-3/4 acre...`

That SAME printed extract's nearby `REF: DEED INDEXES - WELCH` table shows **JOHN** in the **GRANTEE** column with `G:307; K:372`. If accurately transcribed, that means **John Welch received** property associated with this K:372 instrument and James Webb likely transferred it. **The search-result transcription is incomplete; original deed might contain other parties, wife consent, witnesses, grant details, signatures and different recording dates.**

**Critical transcription limits:** The OCCGS PDF is about 30 MB; direct web tool page open could not retrieve it (size error) and local document-download attempt failed. Search engine surfaces the exact excerpt and print p56, but the **layout/pixels have not been visually verified** in this session. Do NOT claim the 1993 source's entire page was read, that the county index manuscript was seen, or that the 1804 original deed was downloaded.

**A small apparent date difference:** The paper's Welch family deed-index table puts JOHN WELCH's entry in a row beside **1806**, while the K:372 **deed is explicitly dated 27 June 1804**. Because these might represent different date types or the table layout may have been flattened in text extraction, **do not claim the instrument was recorded in 1806 or mistakenly change the deed's date** until the deed's register heading is inspected.

**Land description:** part of Lot No. 57 of the “Niels Patent” as transcribed; the spelling could be a reading error for *McNeil's Patent*. Other local histories mention McNeil's Patent, but the 1804 source ORIGINAL must be inspected before changing the actual historical name. Amount **$63.75**, **12¾ acres**, provisional from published abstract.

**Period-correct geography verified independently:** Brookfield was a Chenango County town in **1804**; **Madison County formed 21 March 1806** out of Chenango, including Brookfield. Official Madison County history:
https://www.madisoncounty.ny.gov/212/About-Madison-County
https://www.madisoncounty.ny.gov/1685/Madison-County-History
Thus the 1993 parenthetical “now Madison” does not demonstrate a problem with the 1804 statement.
