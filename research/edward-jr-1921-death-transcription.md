## Evidence decision

The original certificate establishes **11 September 1921**, Murray, Salt Lake County, Utah, as the death of Edward Milo Webb, born 8 March 1847 in Missouri, husband of Ellen Webb, aged 74 years, 6 months, 3 days. The matching birth, spouse and parents identify the direct ancestor; this is not the different Edward Milo Webb born in 1838 or the separate 1927 index entry.

The conflicting **4 April 1924** Church History structured date is superseded for this ancestor by the original civil death record. Retain the conflict history; no FamilySearch or Church History edits were made. Birth and parent information on a death certificate are informant-supplied retrospective evidence, not an original birth record. The unclear paternal middle initial is not used to revise his father's name.

The cause is directly certified as **cancer of stomach**. The physician entered Arizona as where the disease was contracted; preserve that entry without treating it as a modern medical conclusion about cancer's geographic cause. His recorded occupation was **Common Laborer**. Burial is recorded at Holladay Cemetery on 12 September 1921. Hattie Webb is named as informant; her precise relationship is not written in that field.
