## Newly researched German retrieval targets — catalogue only, no name-level entries seen

A. **Civil death in 1883, Großörner:** Preußen civil registration began 1874. Großörner historically had its own Standesamt, and municipal registers are reportedly preserved within the successor city **Mansfeld**. Landesarchiv Sachsen-Anhalt's official guidance says 19th-century post-1874 civil vital registers primarily lie with municipal offices, whereas C 131 church register duplicates run principally to 1874 and C 131 II surviving civil death duplicates chiefly cover later 1933–1945. Do not approach state C 131 II as if it holds the 1883 original.
