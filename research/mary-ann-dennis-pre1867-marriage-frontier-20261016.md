## Question A: reported marriage of Peter John / John Peter Gooley and Mary Ann Dennis, 1866 Alpena, Michigan
- Earlier direct-person audit found NO marriage original or attached 1866 marriage source (marriage asserted on FamilySearch tree). Their household and family relationship are well evidenced later by Michigan child birth entries, 1880 and 1910 censuses, deed signed by Peter and Mary, etc. Those later records do not establish a ceremony date or maiden-family parents.
- **New targeted coverage check (16 Oct):** State returns / FamilySearch “Michigan, Marriages, 1868–1925” start **1868** (https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/1452395), though some county collections cover other years. Alpena County Library's own online **marriage index starts 1870** and library microfilm marriage holdings start **1871** (https://alpenacountylibrary.specialdistrict.org/vital-records). Michigan statewide recording mandated by 1867 law, so **1866 is prior to systematic statewide coverage**. Do not call the marriage disproven by these negative/absent indexes; do not search same out-of-range collection again expecting it to contain 1866.
- MIGenWeb's extracted **Alpena 1867–1868 marriage state returns** list earliest entries from **7 August 1867**; no Gooley/Dennis marriage appears in the 11 listed 1867/1868 records. This is a bounded finding in a **later date period**, not evidence no wedding occurred in 1866. https://www.migenweb.org/alpena/BMD/1867.html
- **Newly identified collections predating 1867:** *Trinity Episcopal Church (Alpena) church records 1865–1978*, and *First Congregational Church (Alpena) church records 1865–1942*. These are catalogued by FamilySearch Library via https://ldsgenealogy.com/MI/Alpena-County-Church-Records.htm ; Alpena County Library also lists Trinity Episcopal 1865–1968 and First Congregational 1860–1976 in its church record holdings https://alpenacountylibrary.specialdistrict.org/vital-records . They MAY contain early ministerial weddings, but **specific 1866 marriage sections and access conditions have not been verified**. Do not assume Church of England recorded for possible Mary in 1851 establishes her adult church or where ceremony occurred.
- **Other paper route:** Alpena *Thunder Bay Monitor*, published 1863–1866, or *Alpena County Pioneer* (began 1866), may carry wedding notices if surviving issues are available. Chronicling America confirms historic titles, not digitized full-text issue availability: https://chroniclingamerica.loc.gov/titles/places/michigan/alpena/alpena/ . LOC microfilm catalogue reports **no microform for the Pioneer** at catalog time, so holdings need verification; not a proved accessible newspaper original.
- Alpena County Clerk's online public search/order is **$16 for certified first copy** (https://www.alpenacounty.org/181/Marriages), but published historical index coverage starts later. **Do NOT order** without first confirming any pre-1867 book exists and user authorizing costs. In-person viewing allowed at clerk office; cameras prohibited https://alpenacounty.org/187/Genealogy .
