## Independent corroboration located in this tranche

**Velmer June Webb, 28 April 1930 – 25 March 2022**, [Owens Livingston Mortuary obituary](https://www.owenslivingstonmortuary.com/obituaries/velmer-webb), identifies his parents as **Jonathan Henry and Della Ray Webb**, his birth on the Webbville ranch between Show Low and Lakeside, and his employment at the family sawmill; it records meeting **Cherie Cluff** after moving to Mesa for sixth grade and marrying her on **26 November 1948**. This independently confirms the interview abstract’s identification of June, family and locality; it does not convert the full oral-history abstract into verified contemporary events.

The ten-page **Irene Webb Merrell** biography already transcribed and published in the Atlas documents the family’s 1912 Mexico exodus and the post-exodus Arizona settlements; see preserved `research/edward-jr-irene-transcript/page-09.txt` and `page-10.txt` (PDF pages 9–10). This confirms the broad flight and relocation era, **not** the interview abstract’s attribution to Villa, alleged George Scott transaction, or later sawmill founding.
