## 2. Diplomatic content and faithful modern rendering

**Original (spelling/capitalization preserved in words; line breaks normalized for reading):**

> MARRIED.
>
> In Ogden city on the 17th inst., by Ezra T. Benson, ISAAC VAN WAGONER CARLING, of Fillmore city, and ASENATH ELIZABETH BROWNING, of Ogden city.

**Transcription caution:** The reproduced page is an original typographic source; the phrase `17th inst.` refers to 17 November **1854**, the issue month. This transcription preserves the substantive wording; punctuation and line breaks follow the legible newspaper printing as closely as the supplied full-page image permits. If a future direct original-image capture offers clearer punctuation, it may be refined without altering the known names/date/place/officiant.

**Museum-standard plain-English reading:** On **17 November 1854 at Ogden City**, Isaac Van Wagoner Carling, described as residing in **Fillmore City**, married Asenath Elizabeth Browning, described as from **Ogden City**. **Ezra T.
