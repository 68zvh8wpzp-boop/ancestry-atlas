## 1. Sources compared without re-reading old census images

**A. Institutional historical profile for Miriam (derivative biographical database).**  
Church History Biographical Database, [Miriam Elizabeth Hobson](https://history.churchofjesuschrist.org/chd/individual/miriam-elizabeth-hobson-1843?lang=eng). Displays:
- Birth **31 August 1843** and death **22 June 1869**;
- Journey with the **Benjamin Gardner Company, 1852**, with her parents Jesse and Catherine Dougherty Hobson, among the named family;
- Marriage to **Isaac VanWagoner Carling dated 27 August 1857**, explicitly displaying her age as **13**.
- Its visible source section links an **1860 Millard County census**, *not* a displayed 1857 marriage license or sealing register. This catalog therefore corroborates the **reported** event in institutional metadata but is **not** proof that curators examined the original 1857 marriage page.

**B. Institutional profile for Isaac (same data ecosystem).**  
[Isaac VanWagoner Carling, RID PER-6SFMYP8](https://history.churchofjesuschrist.org/chd/individual/isaac-vanwagoner-carling-1831?lang=eng) likewise lists **27 August 1857** for his marriage to Miriam. Because both profiles are maintained in the **same source system**, do not count them as two wholly independent eyewitness confirmations.

**C. Compiled family history (internally inconsistent).**  
The freely readable `Isaac Van Wagoner Carling` family-history compilation at https://de.scribd.com/document/48194950/Isaac-Van-Wagoner-Carling contains:
- In its opening family summary: Miriam is second spouse, marriage **27 August 1857**, **Salt Lake City**.
- In the **“Miriam Elizabeth Hobson”** narrative: starts the account by saying Isaac met and married her **in 1859 while in Fillmore**.
- The same narrative then says she married **27 August 1857 at the home of “S. T. Hoot (Hoyt?)” in Fillmore**, **not Salt Lake City**, and calls her **16** at the time of marriage.
- Within that narrative she is also said to have been born **31 August 1843**. **1857 Aug 27** means **13 years, 11 months, 27 days** old, and Isaac (born 30 Nov 1831) was **25**, a roughly **12-year** age difference. **1859 after Aug 31** would produce age 16, closer to its prose, but **1859 is not authenticated** as the actual wedding year.
- “S. T. Hoot (Hoyt?)” is the source's own uncertain reading, not proof of the property owner or officiant, and must never be silently standardized. A historical diary of Wilford Woodruff mentions a **Brother Hoyt in Fillmore**, but no evidence connects that man to this marriage; the similarity is **only a search lead**, not identity confirmation.

**D. Other genealogical compilations (derivative, not contemporaneous).**  
[Frank family pedigree, Isaac and Miriam family group](https://www.frankhistory.com/pedigrees/familygroup.php?familyID=F1611&tree=frank) lists **27 August 1857** in **Salt Lake City** and Miriam birth **31 August 1843**. Another [Hiatt family pedigree](https://www.hiattfamily.net/gen/lda-2014-10-03/aqwg16020.htm) gives Miriam birth as **30 August 1843**, showing a **one-day birthday variant**, but the age-at-marriage result is the same (13 in Aug 1857). None is an 1857 eyewitness or original civil/sealing register.

**E. Historical Hobson genealogy (compiled family line).**  
[Hobson genealogy, p. 38](https://www.seekingmyroots.com/members/files/G003335.pdf) provides **31 August 1843** birth, **1857 marriage to Isaac** (without independent exact date/place in excerpt), **22 June 1869** death and four children (John Henry, Ellen, Lydia, Jesse). The printed page is a genealogical compilation, **not** Miriam's original birth or marriage register; independent origination of its facts is not established.

## 3. Targeted search scope and obstacle, not another census sweep

Searched for the couple and date in 1857 newspaper marriage notices, original-period publicly indexed pages, Church History's person views and marriage/sealing catalog references. **No accessible dated original announcement, individual register folio/image, officiant, license or exact sealed-ordinance identification surfaced**. General 1887+ Salt Lake/Weber county **civil marriage licenses cannot resolve 1857** and were not swept. The early church marriage/sealing originals remain a **likely access-controlled institutional source** requiring signed-in interactive FamilySearch or a Church History Library request, rather than another generic public search. No new newspaper issue/page can be validly fabricated or claimed reviewed.

This was a **single targeted attempt**. Do not spend another tranche rereading those same profiles, the diary, newspaper search terms or census images absent genuinely new access.
