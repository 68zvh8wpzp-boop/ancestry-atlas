## Morten Peder Mortensen

- Original Danish National Archives / Arkivalieronline, Fanefjord Sogn (Præstø Amt), Kontraministerialbog, *Fødte mænd 1825–1838*, image 36/132, manuscript leaf 28, entry 19, visually inspected and saved in private Ancestry Atlas Library audit A015. Name in register: **Morten Pedersen**, birthday **24 May 1828**, baptism **22 June 1828**, parents **Peder Mortensen and Lene Pedersdatter** of Hårbølle. The date is a firsthand original-register reading; do not adopt the derivative May 27 date. Capture quality: two overlapping user-supplied screenshots of the original scan, not an original archive-master file.
- Original contemporary letter by George Teasdale, 8 June 1891, transcript p. 4: funeral held Sunday June 7, with evidence he was alive earlier Saturday June 6. **6 June 1891 strongly supported** as death date. Original manuscript custody Church History Library, Wilford Woodruff stake correspondence files CR 1 161, pp. 33–36, https://wilfordwoodruffpapers.org/documents/f618db4f-756d-4b95-b401-1376bac192f5/page/c052ee91-aa3f-4b0a-9ec3-a30a45ed05cc . The official inquest decision and death manner are **not established**. Do not describe suicide as a confirmed finding.
