## Locked handling rules
- Authentic family photographs take priority over archival context.
- Approved restorations may clean dust, halftone interference, contrast, and minor damage without changing faces, bodies, clothing, objects, or historical content.
- No charcoal is required for the current Marion scene plan.
- Rejected intermediate variants are excluded from release packaging.
- Archival candidates cannot enter production until their public-domain or otherwise rights-cleared provenance is recorded.
- Evidence qualifications remain in the research layer rather than the spoken narration.
