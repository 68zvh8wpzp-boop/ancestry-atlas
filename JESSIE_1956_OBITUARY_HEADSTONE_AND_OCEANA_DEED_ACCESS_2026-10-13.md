## Faithful transcription of the obituary
> **MRS. JESSIE SKINNER**
>
> Funeral service for Mrs. Richard Skinner, aged 76, who died Monday at the home of her daughter, Mrs. James King of Beaver Lake, will be Thursday at 2 at the Spratt Free Methodist church, the Rev. Glenn Vibbert officiating. Interment in Spratt cemetery.
>
> Born Jessie Aurin Oct. 25, 1879 in Oceana county, she was married there to Mr. Skinner in 1899. He died in 1933 and since that time she has made her home with the Kings at Beaver Lake.
>
> Besides Mrs. King, she is survived by two daughters, Mrs. Charles Brenay of Hurricane, Utah and Mrs. Walter English of San Jose, Cal.; two sons, Clifford of Battle Creek and John of Denver, Colo.; two brothers, William and Earl McConnell of Big Timber, Mont.; 21 grandchildren and 26 great-grandchildren.
>
> The body will be at Bannan funeral home until noon Thursday when it will be taken to the church in Spratt.

*Transcription note:* Words have been assembled across newspaper column breaks; abbreviations and names reflect the clipping. Cemetery capitalization standardized only in presentation, not to imply a separate source.

## Evidentiary findings and unresolved conflicts
- Newspaper states **25 October 1879, Oceana County** and **age 76**, aligning with the 1879 Oceana original birth return and known 1899 marriage; **independent obituary support for 1879**.
- **Physical headstone actually reads 1897–1956**. An 1897 birth would imply ~58/59 at 1956 death, incompatible with newspaper age 76 and 1879 birth registration. Keep conflict explicit; do **not** silently “correct” stone or claim burial-book evidence.
- **Monday death** corresponds to **5 March 1956**; **Thursday funeral/interment** is **8 March 1956**, noting announcement is forward-looking and does not independently prove final burial actually occurred that day.
- Family at death: named surviving **Mrs. James King** (Beaver Lake), **Mrs. Charles Brenay** (Hurricane, Utah), **Mrs. Walter English** (San Jose, California), sons **Clifford** (Battle Creek) and **John** (Denver), brothers **William and Earl McConnell** (Big Timber, Montana); **21 grandchildren and 26 great-grandchildren**. This provides new surname/geographic leads. **Do not assign King/English married surnames to a specific given-name daughter without corroboration.** Likewise brother term does not independently distinguish full from half siblings.
- **No father named**. This does not resolve indexed John Auene vs 1880 Jonas Aurin vs proposed Karl Johan Aurén. No parent-child confidence promotion.
