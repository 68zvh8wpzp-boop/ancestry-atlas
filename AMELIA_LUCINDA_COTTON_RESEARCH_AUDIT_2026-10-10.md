## Working dates and places — SECONDARY sources, NOT verified originals
- The modern PeopleLegacy memorial for [Amelia Lucinda Cotton McConnell](https://peoplelegacy.com/amelia_lucinda_cotton_mcconnell-18361j) gives **24 January 1853, Potsdam, New York** to **11 February 1919, Michigan**, burial **Carpenter Cemetery, Walkerville, Michigan**, and names her parents **John West Cotton** and **Betsey Ann Brockway (later Rackliff)**. The service explicitly disclaims verification.
- It assigns children to three surname groups: **George W., Ivah Lenoria, Frances I. Kelley** (1871–77), **Jessie and Simon Aurin** (1879, 1881), and **Earl Rolland McConnell** (1893). Its Jessie entry mistakenly gives **1897** rather than 1879; siblings and marriage sequence are *leads only*.
- The same memorial identifies **Daniel Phillip Kelley** and **Lorenzo Smith McConnell** as spouses, while the Geni profile [Amelia McConnell](https://www.geni.com/people/Amelia-McConnell/6000000009951408617) also lists **Jonas Aurin**. No marriage record has been inspected here. Do not infer any marriage order, divorce, biological paternity, or legal relationship without contemporary evidence.
- The [Harriet Cornelia Cotton Kelley memorial](https://peoplelegacy.com/harriet_cornelia_hattie_cotton_kelley-35242y) lists Harriet (1858–1920) as Amelia’s sister and names both women’s claimed parents John West Cotton and Betsey Ann Brockway. It also places the family from upstate New York into Oceana County.
- The [John West Cotton memorial](https://peoplelegacy.com/john_west_cotton-6H4l7C) gives **30 March 1870** death, Carpenter Cemetery, parents **Solomon Cotton** and **Ruth Montague Cotton**, and seven children including Amelia. Birth year listed **1825**.

## Important older published genealogy — high priority to inspect in original
George William Montague and William Lewis Montague, *History and Genealogy of the Montague Family of America* (Amherst, Mass.: Press of J. E. Williams, **1886**), 785 pp., [public-domain edition at Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL7007770M/History_and_genealogy_of_the_Montague_family_of_America) and [Internet Archive identifier `historygenealogy00mont`](https://archive.org/details/historygenealogy00mont).
- The indexed text snippet of its Cotton family section (near **printed p. 714**, item **2950**) contains **“John West Cotton, b. April 1, 1826, in Edwards, N.Y., m. Nov. 23, 1847…”**. The *full entry has NOT been examined.* It is significant because the 1886 publication predates modern compiled memorials by more than a century.
- That contemporary genealogy may identify John West Cotton’s wife, parents and children, but **no missing details should be invented from an excerpt**. Confirm original book pages and cite page numbers, and compare it with birth, marriage, census, death and cemetery records.
- Its claimed birth **1 April 1826** disagrees with the **1825** memorial birth estimate. Retain discrepancy until a primary source clarifies.
- The separate claims that his parents were **Solomon Cotton and Ruth Montague** must remain *provisional* until full 1886 entry and older records are correlated.
