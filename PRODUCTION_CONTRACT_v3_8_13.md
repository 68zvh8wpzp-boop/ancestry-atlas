# Ancestry Atlas v3.8.13 production contract

This contract supplements `PRODUCTION_CONTRACT_v3_8_0.md`. It updates release
state after the Webb runtime integration and locks reusable production patterns
learned during that branch. It does not alter genealogy or prior approvals.

## Current release state

- Twelve biographies use exact, approved OpenAI Fable recordings.
- The original seven biographies and the five-person Webb branch are registered
  in the live runtime. Approved audio remains authoritative for transcript and
  timing and must never be regenerated as a convenience.
- The Webb runtime contains five biographies and thirty-six approved scenes.
- James Webb Jr. is the supported Webb tour endpoint. James Webb Sr., Elizabeth
  Douglas, Gideon Webb, Edith Bates and the proposed English line remain outside
  the ordinary guided biography sequence.

## Reusable proof-boundary pattern

1. End the supported biography at the last supported person.
2. State the unresolved parent or identity problem plainly.
3. Keep descendant explanation above the proof line or omit it when redundant.
4. Offer any theorized continuation only through an explicit, optional
   `Explore Research Frontier` surface.
5. Candidate people remain provisional or frontier, use dashed relationships,
   and are not counted as established generations.
6. Exiting the frontier returns to the supported endpoint.

The frontier may explain a complete overseas theory only when it also displays
the broken proof link, evidence for and against, material conflicts, and the
records needed next.

## Evidence and research imagery

- A missing record is not a negative record unless the searched collection is
  sufficiently complete to support that conclusion.
- Research-document scenes use actual obtained record images. Do not fabricate
  a register page, repository page, document facsimile or documentary montage.
- A visual must do distinct narrative or evidentiary work. Redundant descendant
  diagrams or decorative evidence panels are struck rather than retained.
- Evidence grades in narration, graph, manifests, captions and visuals must agree.

## Map and label system

- Prefer an approved archival or authoritative geographic base. A project-built
  period-style map is allowed only when its contextual nature is explicit.
- Use one Atlas label system: dark, high-contrast, readable endpoint labels with
  consistent type, hierarchy and placement across maps.
- Solid lines show supported movement. Dotted lines show supported endpoints
  with an unresolved route. A sequence of places never implies exact roads.
- Labels must remain legible at phone and television presentation sizes.

## Flag system

- Verify every flag against the exact scene date and jurisdiction. Check star
  count, star arrangement, canton and adoption period before approval.
- A proof-boundary flag composite is contextual evidence design, not a claim
  that the depicted flags occupied the historical scene.
- Photographic flag treatments may be faded behind evidence hierarchy, but may
  not reduce label legibility or blur the proof line.

## Approval and rights states

- Present one actual visual candidate at a time and ask `Approve or revise?`.
- Crop or zoom revisions preserve the approved concept unless the user changes
  the concept. An approved asset is not regenerated for layout convenience.
- Rights scope is recorded separately as `private`, `public-noncommercial`, or
  `commercially-cleared`. Public display approval does not imply commercial
  reuse permission.
- The 1891 Sarah Elizabeth Carling Webb family photograph is approved for the
  public, noncommercial family Atlas. It remains blocked from commercial use
  until clearance is documented or the image is replaced.
- No commercially distributed package may contain an unresolved commercial
  rights hold.

## Narration pattern

- Narration remains third-person life history, centered on its subject.
- Production commentary about what “the Atlas” chooses belongs in contracts and
  manifests, not narration.
- Do not immediately append methodological qualification after an attributed
  family anecdote unless a genuine conflict changes the story.
- Adjacent biographies share chronology sparingly and never appropriate another
  person's remembered perspective.
- Religious material from Marion and Jay's life-story source remains excluded.

## Scale and release hygiene

- Branch manifests use machine-readable states: `draft`, `in-review`,
  `approved`, `runtime-integrated`, and explicit rights holds.
- The package manifest must enumerate every approved audio file and every loaded
  biography module.
- Verification loads every production module, checks exact transcripts and
  audio paths, counts registered biographies and scenes, and rejects stale
  phrases such as `awaiting runtime` after integration.
- Every completed branch uses the same sequence: evidence register, biography,
  one-at-a-time visuals, audio lock, runtime generation, automated verification,
  live smoke test and deployment.
