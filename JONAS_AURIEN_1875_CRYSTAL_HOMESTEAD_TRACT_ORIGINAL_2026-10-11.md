## Why this is the strongest Jonas discovery since the 1879 marriage

1. **1875 in same specific township as Michigan 1879 originals**:
   - April **13 1875**, “**Jonas Aurien**” records 159.17 acres in **Crystal Township** federal land tract book.
   - **2 Feb 1879**, original Crystal Township Michigan marriage **Jonas Aurin**, Swedish-born farmer aged36, to **Amelia** (later proven Cotton).
   - **25 Oct 1879**, original Crystal birth **Jessie Auene**, father **John Auene**, born Sweden, occupation farmer.
   - **20 Apr 1880**, tract book later notes patent reference, a **five-year period** after land entry. In June1880 original Elbridge census the family is recorded as Jonas Aurin and Amelia L.
   - **29 Nov 1881**, original Elbridge son Julius's birth, father John Aurene.
   This **strongly favors** the 1875 land applicant = Jessie’s father, based on name, chronology, occupation, extremely specific township; not formal absolute identity because tract page lacks age, wife, country.
2. **Homestead-like five-year completion** with a `Hd` label, 159.17 acres, $10 fee and 1880 patent entry: the evidence is **highly suggestive of a federal Homestead Act application**. **Do not assume** homestead without official authority confirmation in patent.
3. **Potential immigration shortcut**: US National Park Service [Homestead Records guide](https://www.nps.gov/home/learn/historyculture/homesteadrecords.htm) says original homestead files can contain residence testimony, named witnesses, family members, service, and for declarant immigrants **naturalization intent and sometimes origin**. Since Jessie’s father was Swedish-born, this could provide missing **first papers, naturalization, origin, residency 1875–1880**, perhaps deposition or identity of neighbors. This **may** bypass incomplete pre1906 county naturalization indexes.
4. NARA [Accessing Land Entry Records](https://www.archives.gov/research/land/accessing-land-entry-files) confirms pre1908 files in RG49 at National Archives, Washington DC, and that to order an **original case file** one needs **state, land office, authority/type of patent, and patent document number**. Order online or [NATF Form84](https://www.archives.gov/research/land/accessing-land-entry-files). This is a *case file*, not merely the one-page patent document. No order placed, no fee authorized.
5. **Next discriminating step**: [BLM General Land Office public patent records](https://www.blm.gov/services/land-records/glo), search with **Michigan, Crystal Township / T16N R16W, section30** and surname variations **Jonas Aurien, Aurin, Aurene, Auren, Auene, Orien** and issue date around **20 April 1880**.
