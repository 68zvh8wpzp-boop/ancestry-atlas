# Ancestry Atlas v3.9.24 mobile scan-preview contract

This amendment applies to every present and future branch. It records the
standing rule for showing source documents reliably on small screens.

## Original-source authority

1. The original scan, PDF, image, title, provenance, and source link remain the
   authoritative evidence. A display derivative must never replace, rewrite,
   crop, or silently downgrade the original record.
2. Tapping a document card must continue to open the original full-resolution
   document or source record, with its provenance intact.
3. A first-page preview is a navigation aid only. It is not a transcript,
   proof decision, identity claim, or substitute for reviewing the complete
   document.

## Mobile preview rule

1. When a source PDF is available but its remote thumbnail is unreliable,
   production may include a locally hosted preview rendered from the genuine
   first page of that same PDF.
2. The preview must show meaningful source content at thumbnail size. Large
   blank margins must be reduced for display when necessary, without changing
   the uncropped original opened by the card.
3. The card must remain semantically labeled as a document/PDF scan. A preview
   must not be presented as a family photograph or as a standalone portrait.
4. If no genuine preview can be made, show a clearly labeled link-only record;
   never fabricate, redraw, or use an unrelated image to fill the card.

## Publication authorization

1. Derived previews may be published to the public Atlas host only after the
   user explicitly authorizes that publication for the relevant source set.
2. That authorization permits publication of the display derivatives only. It
   does not authorize alteration of originals, removal of provenance, or
   broader publication of unrelated family material.
3. For this release, the user explicitly authorized publication of Marion
   Beulah Brenay's eight genuine first-page document previews. The eight
   previews are: 40th anniversary, 50th anniversary, graduation, home,
   letters, “Neat as a Pin,” ration books, and young Marion.

## Asset and count discipline

1. Documents have no per-person cap. The standing photo limit is 30 per person,
   except Sheldon and Inez, who have no photo cap.
2. The preview layer may improve discoverability, but it must not create
   duplicate biography sections or duplicate evidence cards.
3. Religious emphasis in retained text or documents remains reduced unless the
   material predates 1950 or is needed for a specific genealogical claim.

## Release gate

Before release, verify that the live mobile route exposes the expected document
count, that each preview card contains visible source content, and that the
card still opens the original full document. Record the release version and
the preview-to-source mapping in the repository change record.
