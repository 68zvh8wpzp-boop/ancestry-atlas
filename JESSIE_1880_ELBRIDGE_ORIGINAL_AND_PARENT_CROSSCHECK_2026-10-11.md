## Important previously unidentified adjacent-family evidence

The **immediately next household** (dwelling 153, family 167) is Samuel **Rackliff**, wife **Betsey A.**, stepdaughter **Lillie**, and son **Fred. E**. This gives a novel specific cross-check with George W. Montague and W. L. Montague, *History and Genealogy of the Montague Family of America* (1886):

- On printed **p. 714**, its supplement to p. 369's John West Cotton family says **Betsey Ann Brockway**, Amelia Lucinda Cotton's mother, married **Samuel Rackleff on 15 February 1872**, and identifies Amelia as a woman **married to Aurin, residing in Crystal Valley**.
- That 1886 entry says John West Cotton and Betsey Brockway's daughter **Lillie Dale Cotton** was born **8 March 1867**. The 1880 **Lillie, 13**, expressly identified as **Samuel's stepdaughter**, matches her age and expected legal relationship, directly adjacent to **Amelia L. Aurin**, 26. 
- Samuel's **Fred E., 14**, is called **son**, with both parents born Maine, whereas stepdaughter Lillie has parents born New York. This supports the interpretation that the Rackliff household is blended. It does **not** by itself establish Fred's biological mother or conclusively prove Amelia and Betsey's relationship without connecting records.
- This highly distinctive cluster provides materially strengthened evidence that **Amelia L. Aurin = Amelia Lucinda Cotton**, through a mother/stepfather living in the next enumerated dwelling. Nearby dwellings are **not proof that physical houses shared a property**, and the census does not explicitly call Betsey Amelia's mother. Further vital records should independently verify these links.

Historical book full text: https://archive.org/stream/historygenealogy00mont/historygenealogy00mont_djvu.txt . The **printed-page facsimiles pp. 369/714 remain unarchived** as individual images and should be captured; the book text was inspected previously and its publisher/year are verified, but it is historical compiled-family testimony, not a vital certificate.

## Relation confidence and Atlas handling

1. **Directly observed and high confidence:** Jessie (infant) living with **Jonas Aurin** (born Sweden) and **Amelia L.** (born New York), plus Iva and Frances, 19 June 1880.
2. **Strong multi-source working identification:** Amelia L. Aurin in 1880 may be **Amelia Lucinda Cotton**, given the neighboring Samuel/Betsey Rackliff household and 1886 published Cotton/Aurin/Rackleff details.
3. **Still unresolved:** whether 1880 **Jonas Aurin**, 1879 **John Auene**, and FamilySearch linked **Karl Johan Aurén LZD1-X5W** are one man. The 1883 proposed Karl Johan Aurén–Karolina Hult marriage original remains unexamined, a potentially discriminating record.
