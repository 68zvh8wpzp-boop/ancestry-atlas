## Evidence evaluation and genealogical impact

1. Contemporary **29 June 1870 Oceana marriage** original, entry224, identifies **Daniel Kelley**, age24, Michigan-born, married **Amelia L. Cotton**, 17, New York-born, with witness **Betsey A. Cotton**. The **5 May 1871 George W Kelley birth** original identifies parents **D. P. Kelley**, Michigan-born, and **Amelia Kelley**, New York-born, resident Crystal. This is **strong direct and cross-record evidence** for George W as a son of Amelia and the Kelley husband, but **D. P.'s expanded given/middle name is not written on this birth return**.
2. Original 1888 marriage lists Amelia Aurin, maiden **Amelia Cotton**, with **two previous marriages**, to Smith McConnell. Combined with original marriages 1870 Kelley and 1879 Jonas Aurin, the three-marriage documentary sequence remains supported.
3. This specific birth record is **NOT the original 1874/75 birth of Ivah Lenoria Kelley** and is **NOT a birth record for Frances I. Kelley (~1877)**. Those two mothers' and biological fathers' identities are still **open original records**. Both older girls' father-birthplaces were indexed Sweden in 1880 Jonas house, whereas D. P. Kelley born Michigan; do not resolve by inference alone.
4. Personal name conflict: published 1886 Montague family history also reports Amelia's sister **Hattie Cornelia Cotton married a Kelley**. Do not merge Daniel of Amelia's 1870 marriage into Hattie's life without a separate source.
5. **Jonas Aurin / John Aurene** still unsolved in Sweden: born roughly 1843/1844 Sweden per originals in 1879/1880, son Jessie Oct1879, son Julius Nov1881, Amelia next married 1888. Independent Massachusetts Charles Auren, 29 in his original 1882 Worcester marriage, likely distinct, but Jonas's death/divorce/migration/naturalization unknown.
