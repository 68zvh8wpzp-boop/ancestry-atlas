## What is newly established and what is NOT

1. **Twice the same household neighbors, 20 years apart**: In the ORIGINAL **1880 Elbridge census** Jonas Aurin/Amelia L and children were dwelling 152/family166, with **Samuel Rackliff and Betsey A** in the **next enumerated dwelling153/family167**. In this **1900 Elbridge census**, widowed Amelia L. McConnel/Sim and her younger children occupy dwelling83/family86, followed immediately by **Samuel Rackliff and Betsey A** dwelling84/family87. This is an unusually specific independent association strongly supporting Amelia's continuity, maternal family network, and identity.
2. **Independent 1886 published genealogy** (*History and Genealogy of the Montague Family of America*, printed p714) names **Amelia Lucinda Cotton** as married to an **Aurin** in Crystal Valley and identifies her mother **Betsey Ann Brockway** as remarried to **Samuel Rackleff** in 1872, including a daughter Lillie. The 1880 original shows **Lillie 13 explicitly 'stepdaughter'** in Samuel's household. The combined 1880/1886/1900 correlation is **exceptionally persuasive**, though neither census has a column stating Amelia = Betsey's daughter. Avoid saying they lived in physically adjacent houses or on the same property from sequential enumeration alone.
3. **Amelia 'widowed' in 1900** is actually written. It establishes her claim to widowhood **by 7 June 1900**, but **does not identify which husband died**: some compiled sources name **Lorenzo Smith McConnell**, with a McConnel child cohort born around 1889–1893 after Jonas's 1881 child; no original Lorenzo/Amelia wedding/death or Jonas death/divorce entry has yet been read. Do not say Jonas was dead or imply any particular remarriage is proved just because she used the surname McConnel.
4. **Her 10 children born, 8 surviving** is legible, resolves earlier index ambiguity (it is **8**, not **3**), and reveals potential early child deaths worth searching in the 1870s–1890s. Do not equate census motherhood count with 10 biological children of Jonas; children came across surname groupings. 
5. **Father birthplace reported New York for Sim J and for all McConnel children in 1900**, despite the 1879/1880 originals naming **Jessie's** father from Sweden and the 1881 birth original's father birthplace Unknown. The later Sim J birthplace column may be a stepfather/general household substitution, a mistaken answer, or evidence of a different relationship; **cannot choose which** without another original. His name, birth month/year and mother make the Sim J ~ Julius identification very strong.
6. **Mismatches deserve preserving:** 1900 Sim source handwriting shows 18 (born Nov1881), whereas the 1900 index in user screenshot reports age19; the original wins for this row and June1900 arithmetic. Daughter given name/initial and Carl/Earl remain paleographically uncertain. 
7. **Main father-identity conclusion unchanged**: Michigan original Feb1879 marriage names Swedish-born groom Jonas Aurin age36, mother Amelia [surname handwritten disputed, indexed Cotton], October1879 Jessie original father John Auene, June1880 Jonas Aurin census age36, Nov1881 Julius original John Aurene; those likely form a stable Michigan family. Massachusetts 1882 original Charles Auren/Carolina Hult (groom 29, parents Charles/Christine) likely refers to a **different man**, but no formal surname split is proven by the 1900 household.
