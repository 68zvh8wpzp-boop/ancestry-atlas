## Correlation and unresolved readings

Della is not born in Indian Territory here: the visible birthplace is `Old Mex`, with an annotation/overwriting. The attached index's Indian Territory reading is a discrepancy, not a fact to import. No FamilySearch correction made.

The 16-year-old son's name appears `Earl A.` and is indexed that way, whereas the established family child is Karl Albert, born January 1914; matching age and household position support correlation but do not justify silently changing the transcription. The index fragments the household into multiple groups; the original visibly places all ten people together. Thora is not listed in this household; do not infer her location from absence. Velmer June's reported birth later in April follows the enumeration date, so his absence here is expected, not evidence against the ten-child family.

The listed first-marriage ages 22 and 17 agree broadly with the established 1908 marriage narrative, but are not a substitute for its original return. Della's occupation `None` in 1930 differs from `Dressmaker / at Home` in 1920; this does not establish when she stopped sewing or receiving income.
