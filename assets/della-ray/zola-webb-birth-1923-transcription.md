## Correlation and conflict

The birth field clearly appears `July22 1923`, but both filing dates,26February and8March1923, precede July. The family genealogy gives22February1923. Preserve all three entries and seek another original/index correlation; do not silently normalize July to February. Mother31 and father36 fit a February1923 event more closely than a July1923 event, but ages are an inference, not authority to amend this certificate. This scan establishes the recorded parent names, Pinedale residences, Della's ColoniaPacheco birthplace, and seven living children as stated on the return. It does not settle the birth month by itself.

Lottie M.Webb signed the attending physician-or-midwife field and local registrar field. Her credential is not specified in the handwritten entry; the form permits either physician or midwife. Earlier checkpoint calling her definitively a midwife should be read as an unconfirmed inference and is superseded here. Della's separate recollection of assisting births does not make her the attendant in this child's record.
