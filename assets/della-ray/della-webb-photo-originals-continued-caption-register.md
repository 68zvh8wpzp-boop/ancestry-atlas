## Webb family gathering, July 1956

Caption: Several generations gather on the front steps for a family photograph. The printed date reads July, 1956. The contributor identifies Della in the back row and Henry seated among the family.

FamilySearch KWCR-Z55 memory: “Webb Family Gathering”; TH-7727-159582-282629-64. DeEdra Breckenridge, 16 March 2026; upload 0043_a.jpg. Place metadata: Lakeside, Pinetop-Lakeside, Navajo, Arizona. Photographer unknown.

Complete contributor identification text:

This is a copy of a July 1956 photo of the Jonathan Henry and Della Ray Webb family gathered on the front steps. I can identify several people in the picture, though not everyone.

Front row: Marion Webb, Daphne Webb, Drinette Webb.

Second row: Ina Webb, Jay Webb, Jonathan Henry Webb, Renee Webb, Diane Webb.

Back row: Della Webb, Cecil McDowell, Caren McDowell, Ray Webb.

July, 1956
