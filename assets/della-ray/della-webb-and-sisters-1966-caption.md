# Della Webb with her sisters, Wickenburg, 1966

Four sisters stand outside a house in Wickenburg, Arizona. The contributor identifies them, from left to right, as Marguerite Thomas, Fern Willis, Della Webb and Elsie Willis. Della stands slightly forward of the others. The photograph preserves a later-life visit among sisters whose childhood family had moved from northern Mexico to Arizona.
