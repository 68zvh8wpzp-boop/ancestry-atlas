## Caption and correlation

Henry and Della Webb's children invited guests to celebrate their parents' golden wedding anniversary at the L.D.S. Recreation Hall in Lakeside on 16 August 1958, from eight to midnight, requesting no gifts. The announcement prints1908/1958 and corroborates the anniversary year, resolving the retrospective Ellsworth account's1957 discrepancy in favor of this contemporary celebration document. An invitation establishes the scheduled celebration, not independently that every event took place or the identity of attendees. The exact1908 marriage place/officiant remain separate original-record questions.
