## Original

```text
IN MEMORY OF
DELLA RAY WEBB
Born: June 13, 1891, Pacheco, Mexico
Passed Away: December 1, 1969, McNary Arizona

    Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints
                Lakeside Arizona

    Lakeside Cemetery, 3:30 p. m., December 4, 1969
Bishop V. June Webb, officiating

    “Lead Me Gently Home Father”, Grandchildren

Graveside Services: Lakeside Cemetery
Pallbearers: H. Ray Webb, Karl Webb, Maurice Webb,
    Junius Webb, Jay Webb, Bishop V. June Webb
Honorary Pallbearers: Grandsons
```

English original; translation not applicable. Line breaks retained. No illegible text in this scan. Song titles are program entries; no song lyrics transcribed.

## Evidence

The program schedules services at the Lakeside church for 2 p.m. and cemetery interment for 3:30 p.m. on 4 December 1969. It corroborates the death certificate's dates and burial cemetery, and records the intended participants. It does not by itself prove that every scheduled participant performed their role. Six sons are named as pallbearers; grandsons as honorary pallbearers. Grandchildren are listed for a musical number. The program's planned music is consistent with Della's own description of musical family gatherings.
