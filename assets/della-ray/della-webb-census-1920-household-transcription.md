## Correlation

This original records Della's work as a dressmaker at home and the family's Pinedale residence. Her age 28 in January 1920 is consistent with a June 1891 birth; the index's estimated 1892 is not an exact birth date. Immigration `1913` conflicts with Della's own handwritten account of departure on 12 August 1912. Retain both; this enumeration does not settle the crossing date or legal citizenship history. The children's father's birthplace is written Utah despite Henry's Arizona birthplace in his own row; preserve this internal discrepancy. `Carl Albert` is the census spelling of established son Karl Albert; do not rename the canonical person. Employment codes are recorded as written without inferring business income or workplace arrangements beyond the columns.
