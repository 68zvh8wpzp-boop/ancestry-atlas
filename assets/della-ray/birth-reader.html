Translation

Record number / 54. / Birth of / Della Ray / June 17, [1]891. / 9 a.m. Birth record of a natural daughter. In the municipality of Casas Grandes, at eleven in the morning on the twenty-sixth day of June, eighteen hundred and ninety-one, before me, Heraclio Perea [surname reading uncertain], Judge of the Civil Registry, appeared Mr. James Ray, of legal age, a native of the United States and a resident of Colonia Pacheco. He presented a living female child born in the said colony at nine in the morning on the thirteenth day of the current month, whom he named Della Ray, the natural daughter of the declarant, whose name is recorded in this act at his request in accordance with Article eighty of the Civil Code. Mr. James Ray then stated that, concerning the girl presented, he desires [uncertain phrase] to retain Mexican nationality. Witnesses to this declaration were citizens Jesus Martinez and Francisco Varela, of legal age and residents of the locality. The present record was read to the appearing party and witnesses; they stated their agreement and signed. Heraclio Perea [surname uncertain]; James Ray; witnesses Jesus Martinez and Francisco Varela.
