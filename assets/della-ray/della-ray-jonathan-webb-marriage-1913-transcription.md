## Evidence qualifications

- Della's age 21 is entered in Jonathan's affidavit. Her own affidavit's name and age fields are blank; her signature is present. Do not fill blank fields from the index.
- The signed affidavits and filing date are 7 April 1913. This image does not establish the date of a Webb/Ray ceremony or temple sealing.
- Jonathan's age and residence entries are shadowed; no indexed value is promoted as a checked original reading.
- The printed single/unmarried declarations require correlation with the family's documented 16 August 1908 marriage tradition. They are reproduced as document wording, without resolving that conflict.
- The facing license names a different couple. Its 9 April 1913 return, officiant and witnesses must not be assigned to Webb/Ray.
- The original has dark gutter shadow and identification cards obstructing portions of the form. Qualified transcription is complete within that limitation; unreadable portions remain explicit.
