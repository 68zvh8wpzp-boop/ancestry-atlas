## Correlation and cautions
- Person match is strong: full maiden/married name, exact birth and death dates, Pacheco Mexico, husband and son align with the retained family histories.
- Death place is **McNary General Hospital, McNary, Apache County**. Usual residence is **Lakeside, Navajo County**. Do not conflate residence with death location.
- Burial is **4 December 1969, Lakeside Cemetery**, Lakeside. Plot not provided.
- Certificate records 57 years in both town and state. Subtracting from 1969 suggests approximately 1912. This is retrospective informant evidence, not an exact border-crossing date, and the identical town duration needs comparison with documented Thatcher/Pinedale residences.
- Father is written **James Willford Webb**, conflicting with **James Wilford Ray** in the family histories. Retain the actual wording and do not alter the lineage from this isolated surname error. Further parental records remain necessary.
- Mother is **Margrett Mortensen** as typed, compatible with Elsie Margaret Mortensen but not her complete name. Spouse is typed **Johnathon**; preserve that variant in transcription, retain established Jonathan in narrative.
