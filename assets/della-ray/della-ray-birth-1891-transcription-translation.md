## Translation — full English block

*Record number / 54. / Birth of / Della Ray / June 17, [1]891. / 9 a.m.*

*Birth record of a natural daughter. In the municipality of Casas Grandes, at eleven in the morning on the twenty-sixth day of June, eighteen hundred and ninety-one, before me, Heraclio Perea [surname reading uncertain], Judge of the Civil Registry, appeared Mr. James Ray, of legal age, a native of the United States and a resident of Colonia Pacheco. He presented a living female child born in the said colony at nine in the morning on the thirteenth day of the current month, whom he named Della Ray, the natural daughter of the declarant, whose name is recorded in this act at his request in accordance with Article eighty of the Civil Code. Mr. James Ray then stated that, concerning the girl presented, he desires [uncertain phrase] to retain Mexican nationality. Witnesses to this declaration were citizens Jesus Martinez and Francisco Varela, of legal age and residents of the locality. The present record was read to the appearing party and witnesses; they stated their agreement and signed. Heraclio Perea [surname uncertain]; James Ray; witnesses Jesus Martinez and Francisco Varela.*

## Evidence and unresolved readings

- The main body explicitly places the birth in **Colonia Pacheco**, not merely Casas Grandes. Casas Grandes is the registration municipality.
- Main body birth: **13 June1891 at9a.m.** Registration: **26 June1891 at11a.m.** The marginal annotation appears to read **17 June**. Preserve both; do not silently normalize the margin. The main body agrees with the later handwritten life sketch and death certificate.
- Father/declarant **James Ray**, of legal age, native of United States, resident Colonia Pacheco. The document has his signature. It does not supply an exact birthplace, age, middle names, occupation or a mother's name.
- “Hija natural” is the record's civil classification for a child born outside a legally recognized marriage. It does not, by itself, establish the validity or absence of a religious marriage, identify the mother, or settle plural-family relationships. Other evidence establishes Elsie Margaret Mortensen as Della's mother; that name cannot be inserted into this transcription.
- The nationality statement is preserved as historical wording. Its unclear connecting phrase remains qualified. No legal conclusion about later citizenship, citizenship continuity or inheritance is drawn. Her1969 death certificate's U.S.A. entry represents a separate record at a different time.
- Act54 is complete only with the continuation on p.253. Keeping only the first spread would omit witnesses, signatures and the nationality statement.
- A small stroke after “y” near the main body's year boundary is treated as part of the handwriting, not an additional word. Punctuation and word division are transcribed to the extent securely legible. Registrar surname and the nationality connecting phrase require further paleographic checking.
