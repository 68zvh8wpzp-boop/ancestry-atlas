English translation

Number 153
Birth of the
girl Lois
Webb.
Number one hundred fifty-three. In Casas
Grandes at 12 noon on the four-
teenth of December 1898, one thousand eight hundred
ninety-eight, before me, Eduardo Ber-
múdez, Civil Registrar, appeared
the foreigner A. M. T[illegible]ey, a native
of Iowa, United States of North America,
54, fifty-four years of age,
married, residing in Colonia Du-
blán, and presented a living girl
born in that same colony at
9, nine in the morning, on the fourth
of last November, to
whom he gave the name Lois Webb,
legitimate daughter of the foreigner E. M.
Webb, native of Iowa, 51, fifty-
one years of age, married,
schoolteacher, and of his
wife Mrs. Ella Webb, originally from En-
gland, Great Britain, 44, forty-
four years of age, married,
both residing in the aforementioned
Colonia Dublán. Witnesses to
this act were Citizen [illegible]
[illegible] J. L[illegible] and Aurelio Ar[illegible],
adults, married, farmer and
schoolteacher respectively, residents of
this place, domiciled in the first
section of this municipal seat.
This act having been read to them, they
signed in agreement. I attest.
Citizen Civil Registrar [abbreviated]
Eduardo Bermúdez
