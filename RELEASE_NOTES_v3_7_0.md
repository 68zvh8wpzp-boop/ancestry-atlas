# Ancestry Atlas v3.7.0

Input and rollback package: `Ancestry_Atlas_v3_6_0_FLAT_GitHub_Package.zip`.

This release adds a modular direct-video television presentation for the completed James Sheldon Webb biography. Choosing Webb and then **Play on Television** opens a standard 1920×1080 H.264/AAC film. On compatible Apple devices, **Choose Television** invokes the native media playback destination picker; HDMI users can choose **Play Full Screen**. The television receives the film rather than a mirror of Safari, so browser tabs, address bars, the Atlas tree, the research legend, and web controls are not part of the television picture.

The film uses the approved `James_sheldon_webb.mp3`, all 12 approved scenes, the approved scene order, the same transcript-weight timing algorithm used by the interactive Atlas, exact optional narration captions, the Arizona flag, regional locator, compact captions, person name, and year. The first family photograph is deliberately cropped only in the rendered film to exclude surrounding PDF page text; its approved source asset is untouched.

The existing interactive tour, TV Explore Mode, genealogy, evidence states, research notes, camera behavior, and ordinary navigation are unchanged.
