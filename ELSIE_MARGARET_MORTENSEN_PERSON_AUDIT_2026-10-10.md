Reported biography in Source A:
- Born **16 August 1864 Gunnison, Utah** to **Morten Peder Mortensen and Dorothea Knudsen Jensen**; sister **Maria Helena Mortensen**. Father's family traced through Denmark; earlier ancestors not audited as part of this Elsie pass.
- Family relocated **October 1876** toward Arizona territory when Elsie was about twelve. Account recalls sheep, gardens, spinning/wool, dancing, farm work, music.
- Married **James Wilford Ray 27 January 1882** in St. George, Utah (family book date, not original certificate).
- James separately married younger sister **Maria Helena Mortensen 11 December 1884** (family book date). **Two distinct wives / two distinct mothers**, not a single woman under two names.
- Moved from Utah/Arizona to **Colonia Díaz, Mexico** with James and then through **Pacheco** (where daughter Della was born 13 June 1891), **Oaxaca** and **Morelos** (about 1900). Her household is stated to have raised **twelve children**, requiring independent birth/source verification and careful distinction from Maria Helena's children.
- Family's **1912 departure from Mexico** described directly for Elsie Margaret's household, then Tucson/Binghampton and toward Chandler in 1914. Two household residences were provided in Arizona after settlement.
- Reported death **29 June 1918 in Chandler, Maricopa County, Arizona**, after an operation for gallstones. Not original death certificate.

**Source B (stronger *authored* family participant perspective).** [Nellie Ray Toone (James Ray and Maria Helena's daughter), *James Wilford Ray: A Biography*, signed June 1974, FamilySearch memory no.71246447](https://www.familysearch.org/da/memories/memory/71246447/James%2BWilford%2BRay%2Bby%2BNellie%2BRay%2BToone). Nellie's preface says she combined Maria Helena's stories, siblings' experiences, her own final-twelve-years personal recollections and printed historical sources. **Therefore household descriptions from early Mexico are derivative family memory; later Chandler accounts are near participant recollection.**
- Says marriage Elsie/James **28 Jan 1882**, marriage Maria/James **3 Dec 1884** — *both differ* from printed family book's **27 Jan 1882 / 11 Dec 1884**. Need two original marriage entries to decide. These are not evidence of two separate couples with identical names.
- Repeatedly differentiates children by mother, and says the two women had separate dwellings in later Arizona. Della (Margaret) and Ada (Maria) born about seven weeks apart at Pacheco in 1891.
- Notes two family houses on Ray Road after 1915; Margaret's health limited activity; source recalls household sewing, clothes, nursing through final illness.
- Narrates Margaret's fatal sickness and gallstones operation and gives **29 June 1918**, agreeing with Source A on day. The **actual original civil death record was not viewed**.
- Nellie says Della lived at Pinedale in **1919**, and later cared for younger brother Waldo after James's 1920 death; keep those on Della's narrative rather than repeating whole scene on Elsie.
