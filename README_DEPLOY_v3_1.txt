New interaction model
- The guided story opens in slideshow-first / cinema mode.
- The photo + caption stays in view while Alfie reads.
- Scene changes happen on paragraph boundaries (or roughly every 1–2 paragraphs).
- A persistent 'Hide slideshow' control collapses the slideshow and reveals the synchronized highlighted transcript.
- When the slideshow is hidden, highlighted text auto-follows Alfie.
- Tap 'Show slideshow' to return to the visual experience.
- Map + period-appropriate Arizona flag plates are part of every scene.
- The locator label updates by scene.
