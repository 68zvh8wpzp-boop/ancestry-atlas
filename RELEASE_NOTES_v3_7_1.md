# Ancestry Atlas v3.7.1

Input and rollback package: `Ancestry_Atlas_v3_7_0_FLAT_GitHub_Package.zip`.

This corrective release removes the ambiguous route that allowed James Sheldon Webb's completed biography to fall through to the legacy interactive slideshow. The Webb viewing choice now has three explicit actions:

- **Watch Full Screen** opens the approved 1920×1080 biography film and immediately requests the device's native video full-screen presentation. On iPhone and iPad, this is the supported route that removes Safari's address and tab bars from the picture.
- **Play on Television** opens the same approved film and invokes Apple's native AirPlay playback-destination picker when Safari exposes it. The television receives the film rather than a mirror of the browser interface.
- **Explore Story & Transcript** preserves the prior synchronized interactive slideshow and exact transcript for users who deliberately want that mode.

Other family lines retain their existing interactive tours. The 3D Atlas, genealogy, evidence states, research notes, navigation, camera behavior, approved narration, approved scene sequence, and source assets are unchanged.
