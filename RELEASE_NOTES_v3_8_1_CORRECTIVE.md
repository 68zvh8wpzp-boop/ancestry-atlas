# Ancestry Atlas v3.8.1 — mobile presentation corrective checkpoint

This release preserves the v3.8.0 cross-platform architecture and corrects issues found in real iPhone testing.

- Mobile family view now prioritizes a direct-ancestor ladder with the current focus at the foot of the view.
- Story-to-story transitions reset their scene state and cannot be interrupted by optional map/flag rendering.
- Story exit restores labels, selection, camera context, and mobile navigation history.
- The approved restored Jay and Marion photograph replaces the page-text crop in Sheldon’s opening scene.
- Geography is a larger floating card over the photograph and can be opened as a larger map detail.
- The family-line chooser has a persistent visible close control.
- Album and document surfaces suppress the tree dock and the Album includes approved biography photographs.
- Mobile Back accurately reflects whether navigation history exists.

Only the Arizona flag is presently considered user-approved. Other map and flag treatments remain candidates for one-at-a-time review. Fable narration remains pending and no browser speech fallback is present.
