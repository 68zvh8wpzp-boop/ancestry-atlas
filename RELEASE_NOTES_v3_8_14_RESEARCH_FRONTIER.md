# Ancestry Atlas v3.8.14 — Webb Research Frontier

Approved 29 August 2026.

## What changed

- Added **Explore Research Frontier** at the final supported Webb tour stop, James Webb Jr.
- Kept the Research Frontier outside the ordinary guided biography sequence and behind explicit visitor choice.
- Added a separate full-screen evidence surface with the approved faded England and Grand Union flag context.
- Distinguished the supported endpoint with a solid blue stage, candidate relationships with dashed amber stages, and the unresolved English horizon with a dashed pink stage.
- Added evidence supporting the theory, limits and conflicts, the broken proof link, and the next original records needed.
- Returning from the frontier exits to the supported James Webb Jr. Atlas position.
- Added a data generator so future branch frontiers can use the same contract without duplicating runtime markup.
- Gated incomplete branch frontiers out of the interface until they contain evidence for, evidence against, and a records-needed plan. The incomplete Canada frontier therefore remains unavailable.

## Evidence boundary

The supported public Webb tour still ends at James Webb Jr. James Webb Sr., Elizabeth Douglas, Gideon Webb, Edith Bates, the older New England chain, and the proposed England extension remain candidate research only and are not promoted into the established pedigree.

## Rights boundary

This release retains the public, noncommercial family-use approval for the Sarah Elizabeth Carling Webb family photograph. Commercial reuse remains blocked until that image is cleared or replaced. The commercial viability and licensing audit remains a separate post-tree workstream.
