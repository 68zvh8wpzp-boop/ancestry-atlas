# Production Contract v3.9.25 — Second-pass person pages

This addendum governs the second-pass person pages audited after the Marion Beulah Brenay update.

## Required person-page standard

- Use one coherent third-person museum-style biography. Do not duplicate the same biography under separate headings or merge distinct purposes into repeated prose.
- Expand a life when the evidence supports it: childhood, household, work, education, service, marriage, parenthood, place, later years, legacy, and open questions should be represented when documented. A short life or thin record remains concise; never pad a page or invent detail to meet a word count.
- Name spouses, children, parents, and other relatives when the attached record supports them. Preserve uncertainty and conflicting readings instead of silently reconciling them.
- Keep direct records, original images, family captions, page-level metadata, and research leads visibly distinct.

## Media and document standard

- Documents are uncapped. Photos are capped at 30 per person unless the person is Sheldon or Inez, for whom no photo cap applies.
- Classify an item by its evidentiary role, not by whether its thumbnail happens to be an image. Birth, marriage, census, military, newspaper, permit, marker, and scanned-memory records belong under Documents.
- A document preview must be a genuine first-page or source-image preview with meaningful content. Blank, dark, broken, or placeholder thumbnails are not acceptable.
- A derived preview is display-only. The original PDF, full-resolution image, or source record remains authoritative and must remain linked from the evidence modal.
- When no reliable preview can be retained, use a link-only document card rather than a fabricated or misleading image.
- Keep thumbnails legible in mobile strips; avoid excessive white space where a safe thumbnail crop can improve recognition without altering the authoritative file.

## Content emphasis and publication

- Reduce religious emphasis in post-1950 text and documents unless the item is directly necessary to establish a genealogical relationship. Pre-1950 religious or parish material may remain when genealogically useful.
- Do not guess a public or private identity for a living person. If a standalone profile is unresolved, describe the person through the attached evidence and state the boundary.
- Before publishing any newly derived public preview, retain the original source, provenance, and user authorization. The user has authorized the current Atlas publication workflow.

## Audited scope

This standard applies to Marion Beulah Brenay, James Wilford “Jay” Webb, James Sheldon Webb, Jamar Webb, Jonathan Henry Webb, and Inez Karen Prather, and is the default contract for future second-pass person pages.
