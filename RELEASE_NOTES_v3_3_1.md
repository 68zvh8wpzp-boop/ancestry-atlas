# Ancestry Atlas v3.3.1

Input package: `Ancestry_Atlas_v3_3_0_FLAT_GitHub_Package.zip`.

## Corrected

- Moved television presentation selection into the Guided Audio-Visual Family Tour flow, after the family line is chosen.
- Removed the television entry button from the ordinary Atlas toolbar and mobile tree menu.
- Corrected the narration-control positioning bug that stretched the control panel between both the top and bottom viewport edges.
- Restored the slideshow as the primary Story view; the synchronized transcript remains available through Hide slideshow.
- Added a phone/tablet landscape handoff. In portrait, TV Mode waits and begins the chosen tour only after the device is rotated horizontally.
- Tightened safe-area sizing for phone landscape while retaining responsive 1080p and 4K television layouts.

## Preserved

- Genealogy data, research notes, confidence states, provisional findings, camera behavior, narration text, biography timing, and approved visual assets remain unchanged.
- `James_sheldon_webb.mp3` remains the locked Azure Alfie narration source. The approved MP3 was absent from both the v3.2.0 and v3.3.0 input packages and was not replaced or regenerated.
