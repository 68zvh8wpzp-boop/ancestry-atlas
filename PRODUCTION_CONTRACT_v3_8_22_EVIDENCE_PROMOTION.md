# Ancestry Atlas v3.8.22 evidence-promotion contract

This amendment applies to every present and future branch, not only the Webb line.

## Claim separation

1. Treat each genealogical proposition as a separate claim: a person's identity,
   a marriage, a residence, a parent-child link and an overseas connection are
   not interchangeable.
2. A record proving that a namesake person or couple existed does not prove that
   the person or couple belongs in the direct ancestral line.
3. Compatible names, dates and geography may raise a candidate's priority, but
   may not promote the candidate to supported or confirmed status by themselves.
4. Conflicting identities must remain separate until the conflicting dates,
   spouses, residences and record clusters are reconciled.

## Promotion gate

1. No parent-child edge may move above provisional without direct evidence or a
   documented correlation of independent records that excludes reasonable
   competing identities.
2. Each promotion must identify the exact claim proved, the source class, the
   source's limitations and the reasoning that joins the records.
3. A published transcript is stronger than an unsourced tree but remains a
   derivative source when the original register image has not been inspected.
4. A finding aid or index is a retrieval lead. It does not inherit the facts that
   may be contained in the underlying record.
5. Missing records and gaps in surviving registers are not negative evidence
   unless the record set is shown to be complete for the relevant event.

## Proof-boundary presentation

1. The public tree and biography must stop at the last supported person.
2. Candidate people may appear only in the explicit Research Frontier, with
   dashed relationships, candidate language and the canonical dimmed evidence
   treatment.
3. National flags and overseas imagery may indicate the direction of a research
   question; they may not imply that an unproved migration or ancestry is fact.
4. When new evidence strengthens a candidate without proving the relationship,
   update the evidence panel and retrieval queue but do not move the public proof
   boundary.

## Scalable authoring behavior

The eventual authoring product must store claims separately from people, attach
sources and conflicts to the precise claim they address, show why a confidence
level was assigned, and block publication when a proposed edge lacks its required
evidence review. A reviewer must be able to approve a new source without
silently promoting the family tree or accidentally approving the ancestral
relationship.
