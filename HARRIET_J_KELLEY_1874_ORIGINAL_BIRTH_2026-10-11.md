## Genealogical impact / interpretation boundaries

1. We now have directly inspected original records for **1870 Daniel Kelley and Amelia L. Cotton marriage** in Oceana, **1871 George W. Kelley** birth (father **D. P. Kelley**, mother Amelia Kelley), and **1874 Harriet J. Kelley** birth (father **Daniel P. Kelley**, mother Amelia Kelley). The consistent Crystal residence, identical paternal/maternal birthplaces (MI/NY) and occupation farmer make this a strongly proved early Kelley family grouping.
2. **Identity conflict, do NOT force an alias:** Original **1880 Jonas Aurin household** includes girl **Iva** age **5** (born Michigan, father’s reported birthplace Sweden) and **Frances** age **3**. Harriet J. Kelley born **September 1874** would indeed be **five years old in June 1880**; **the age and female sex fit Iva**. But *Harriet J.* is not *Iva/Ivah Lenoria* and the biological father's birthplaces conflict (Daniel Michigan vs 1880 “Sweden” for Iva). Possibilities: Harriet went by a different name/Iva; she was a different child who was absent from that household; the records were later attached to the wrong Amelia; death before 1880; or age/name copying errors. No documentary proof yet discriminates. Do NOT join Harriet and Iva profiles, and do not label her dead. Follow original later censuses, Ivah marriage/death, 1874/75 births and 1900 Amelia 10 born/8 living.
3. **1886 Montague family history** identifies Amelia Lucinda Cotton married Aurin and her mother Betsey Ann Brockway remarried Samuel Rackleff; same Betsey witness on 1870 Daniel–Amelia original wedding, and same Rackliff household directly after Amelia in BOTH 1880 and 1900 originals. This corroborates Amelia's continuing identity through Kelley → Aurin → McConnel but does not resolve the Harriet vs Iva name discrepancy.
4. The **1888 original Amelia Aurin (maiden Amelia Cotton) → Smith McConnell wedding** reports **two earlier marriages**, consistent with 1870 Kelley and 1879 Jonas Aurin. No actual original tells us if/when Jonas died, divorced or emigrated. The 1882 Worcester original **Charles Auren 29** married Carolina Hult likely another man, **not conclusively** identified as separate without Jonas's subsequent life.
5. **Main paternal ancestor inquiry stays Jonas/John Aurin (~1843–1844 Sweden)** rather than expanding unrelated Kelley branches.
