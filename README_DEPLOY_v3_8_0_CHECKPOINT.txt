ANCESTRY ATLAS v3.8.0 — CROSS-PLATFORM VISUAL CHECKPOINT

STATUS
This is a deployable visual/usability checkpoint. The approved OpenAI Fable
recordings are still pending. All seven completed biography texts and their
approved visual sequences can be previewed without audio. No browser-generated
voice and no legacy Alfie media are included.

DEPLOYMENT
Upload the contents of this ZIP to the same static host and folder used by the
prior production package. index.html must remain at the deployment root. Keep
all biography and asset folders in their packaged relative locations.

IPHONE OR IPAD
The ordinary Atlas works in portrait or landscape. For the cleanest installed
full-screen presentation, use Safari Share > Add to Home Screen and launch the
Atlas from its Home Screen icon. The installed Atlas no longer forces landscape.

TELEVISION
Choose Guided Audio-Visual Family Tour, choose a family line, then choose Present
on Television. Use Control Center > Screen Mirroring for interactive exploration.
Safari controls its own browser bars; the webpage cannot remove them or confirm
that mirroring is connected. An HDMI-connected display can use the same mode.

VISUAL PREVIEW
When narration is pending, the person card offers Preview Story · audio pending.
Use the separate left/right image arrows to inspect approved scenes. These arrows
will disappear when approved Fable audio becomes the scene-timing authority.

ROLLBACK
The prior v3.7.5 working package and its Alfie media remain untouched outside
this checkpoint. Redeploy the prior production ZIP to roll back.
