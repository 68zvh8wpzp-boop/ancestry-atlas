# Ancestry Atlas v3.8.0 production contract

This contract supplements and supersedes stale release-state notes in earlier
packages. It does not alter genealogy, evidence, or approved visual decisions.

## Narration

- Approved voice for every completed biography: OpenAI Fable using `gpt-4o-mini-tts`.
- Locked direction: calm, composed British documentary narration; natural
  conversational cadence; measured but not slow; warm without sentimentality;
  neutral, grounded and quietly authoritative; restrained emphasis and brief
  meaningful pauses; no announcer voice, theatricality, exaggerated emotion or
  robotic regularity.
- Each biography remains a separate modular MP3.
- Sheldon’s compatibility filename remains `James_sheldon_webb.mp3`.
- Approved audio is authoritative. Displayed narration must match it exactly.
- No browser speech, synthetic fallback, or unapproved replacement recording.
- This checkpoint contains exact approved photo-context texts but deliberately
  leaves all seven biographies in a pending-Fable state until recordings are
  generated, fully heard and approved.
- The prior Azure Alfie package remains untouched for rollback. Alfie is not an
  active narrator in v3.8.0.

## Narration construction

- The approved approximately 3,000-character biography remains the anchor.
- Selective approved-photo context may be woven into the existing paragraphs.
- Family photographs may be identified where useful.
- Archival images must be described as historical context without implying that
  the ancestor appears in them.
- Narration must work as audio alone and must not say “as you can see.”
- Do not narrate every map, flag or image.
- Local setting, wider historical context and the supported driver of every
  substantial move are structural requirements.
- Distinguish the reason for a move from conditions encountered after arrival.
- Unresolved drivers remain unresolved in manifests and evidence displays;
  editorial reasoning stays out of natural spoken narration.
- Narration tells the life directly. Production-method commentary such as what
  “the Atlas” chooses to include, omit, infer or avoid belongs in contracts,
  manifests and evidence notes—not in the spoken biography.
- Adjacent biographies may share essential events, but each must remain centered
  on its own subject. Do not repeat another person's remembered scenes or
  perspective when a concise relationship reference will carry the chronology.
- When family memory or another non-primary account is explicitly attributed in
  the narration, that attribution is normally sufficient. Do not immediately
  follow an anecdote with commentary about what its source proves, fails to
  prove, or contains. Put that methodological qualification in evidence notes
  unless a genuine source conflict materially changes the narrated story.
- Religious material from Marion and Jay’s life-story source remains excluded.

## Visuals and rights

Priority is locked:

1. Authentic family photograph.
2. Rights-cleared archival image.
3. Charcoal only when neither is available.

- Do not introduce an image requiring additional permission.
- Approved assets are never regenerated merely for a layout or path check.
- Charcoal remains wide, observational, clearly hand-drawn and restrained, with
  distant ambiguous people and no invented recognizable family likeness.
- Context images must never imply that an ancestor is pictured.
- Family photographs retain their identity, provenance and deliberate crop.
- Rejected charcoal and rejected candidate imagery remain outside the package.

## Story presentation (v3.8.8 clarification)

- Guided Audio-Visual Family Tour remains slideshow-first.
- The selected family line approaches from the same precomputed final camera
  over about thirteen seconds. The same faint node cluster reads first as one
  distant star, then resolves continuously; a separate star layer or handoff
  camera is forbidden because it creates a visual jump.
- Story photographs and captions remain unobscured. A plainly labeled `Place`
  control opens a dedicated full-screen historical place surface containing the
  period flag, meaningful archival regional map, marker, caption and provenance.
- Hide slideshow reveals the exact highlighted transcript; Show slideshow
  restores the visual stage.
- Recorded audio starts paused with sound on.
- Transport keeps its familiar symbols and also names each action: Previous,
  Play/Pause, Narration On/Off, Next and Exit Story.
- While Fable is pending, separate labeled image arrows permit visual review.
  They disappear when approved audio becomes the scene-timing authority.
- Missing media fails quietly, exposes no technical filenames and never triggers
  generated substitutions.

## Maps, flags and movement

- Maps explain place, route and scale rather than acting as generic state icons.
- Preserve terrain, water, relevant period borders and enough regional context.
- Solid routes represent supported movement.
- Dotted routes represent supported endpoints with an unresolved path.
- Important move drivers belong in the biography/evidence data.
- Use the period-appropriate jurisdictional flag for the scene.
- Maps and flags never cover faces or story captions. They open together in the
  dedicated Place view and must include a visible exit.
- Do not fabricate generic locator art when an archival locator has been
  approved. Arizona story scenes use the 1919 AAA Arizona–New Mexico map held by
  the Library of Congress; the Arizona state flag is labeled adopted 1917.

## Mode and media separation (v3.8.8)

- Story and Tree are distinct modes with context-specific controls.
- Phone Tree controls are Story, Center Person, Branch Names, Wider Tree, Home
  and More. Previous Person, media collections and evidence remain in More.
- Family Photos contains portraits and genuine family photographs only.
- Documents contains archival record images only.
- Places contains landscapes, historical scene photographs, flags, maps and
  geographic context. Context imagery may never be labeled a document.

## Cross-platform experience

- Responsive behavior is an optional presentation layer over the existing Atlas.
- Phone portrait, phone landscape/AirPlay, tablet, desktop, 1080p and 4K layouts
  are explicitly tested.
- The measured visible viewport is used when browser chrome reduces available space.
- Safari chrome is controlled by Safari. The webpage does not claim to remove it,
  detect mirroring or disconnect AirPlay.
- Landing and chooser surfaces must not be covered by tree controls.
- The phone tree defaults to a calm local family neighborhood and uses available
  vertical space. Century markers are hidden where they add no useful information.
- The evidence key and research legend begin closed but remain accessible.
- Story photographs retain aspect ratio and historically important content is not
  stretched or arbitrarily cropped.
- Reduced motion, keyboard access, visible focus and safe-area margins remain supported.

## Navigation

- Rotation remains lateral only.
- Rotation pivots around the visible screen center/current point of interest.
- Panning followed by rotation never snaps back to `Me`.
- Users can surf upward through any mapped branch.
- Closing a card does not clear the selected person.
- Phone Back history includes direct node selections.
- Biography exit restores the selected person and prior camera context.
- Entering/exiting TV Mode does not reload the Atlas or discard branch position.

## Evidence and genealogy

- Never invent facts or promote a hypothesis to established ancestry.
- Preserve confirmed, strongly supported, provisional, speculative and unresolved states.
- Preserve research notes, sources, dates, locations, warnings and citizenship-proof targets.
- Do not duplicate the genealogy dataset inside biography modules.
- After a supported branch ending, an optional `Explore Research Frontier`
  surface may present preserved candidate lines without promoting them. Webb
  may extend visibly toward the proposed English line; Canada may extend toward
  the Gooley/Goulet and possible French connection. Both must remain separate
  from ordinary ancestor biographies and use the canonical evidence states.
