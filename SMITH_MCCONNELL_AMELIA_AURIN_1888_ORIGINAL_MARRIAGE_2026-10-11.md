## Genealogical implications (do not overstate)
1. **Documentary Amelia surname chain**: an Amelia recorded as **Aurin**, born NY, age35, and whose birth surname **Cotton** is expressly written, marries **Smith McConnell** in 1888. This independently connects the Michigan 1879 Jonas/Amelia marriage and 1900 Amelia L McConnel household (age47, NY-born, widow, 10 children born/8 living).
2. **Two previous marriages**, per registrar, is primary testimony of reported marital history by Oct1888: (i) Jonas Aurin married Amelia Feb1879 is original-examined; (ii) the attached **1870 Amelia L Cotton/Daniel Kelley** FamilySearch marriage source is now **top priority original**. Do NOT claim Kelley marriage verified just from the source title.
3. This makes the older 1880 girls Iva (~1875) and Frances (~1877) plausibly from an earlier marriage, but their biological father remains unresolved without their original births. Modern family sites misattribute sister Hattie Cotton Kelley and Amelia's relationships; be especially careful.
4. Amelia's **1900 widowhood** may reflect Smith/Lorenzo Smith McConnell dying before 7 June 1900. **No death record verified**; do not deduce Jonas's death from 1900.
5. The 1888 groom appears **Smith McConnell**, a likely lead for the compiled Lorenzo Smith McConnell; whether Smith was his forename, middle name, or another person **requires independent record**. Do not silently rewrite original groom as Lorenzo.
6. Jonas / John Aurin (Sweden-born man c1843–44) still lacks an original naturalization/arrival/death/divorce record. The Massachusetts 1882 Charles Auren (29, parents Charles/Christine) likely a separate person, and should not be attached to Jessie's direct ancestry without explicit linkage.
