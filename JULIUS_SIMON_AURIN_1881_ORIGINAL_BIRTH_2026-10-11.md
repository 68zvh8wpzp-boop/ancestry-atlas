## Why this matters for Jessie's father

Three original primary records are now visually examined:

1. **25 October 1879 Jessie birth**, Crystal, Oceana, original p276 entry16, names **John Auene** and Amelia Auene, father born **Sweden**, mother born **New York**, father farmer; original screenshot archived in Library and analyzed in `JESSIE_1879_ORIGINAL_BIRTH_RETURN_2026-10-11.md`.
2. **19 June 1880 Elbridge census**, original ED216 p16 lines42–50, **Jonas Aurin** age36, farmer born **Sweden**, wife Amelia L.26 born **NY**, infant Jessie, Iva and Frances. Next household Samuel/Betsey A. Rackliff with Lillie stepdaughter, strongly cross-correlating 1886 Montague book's Amelia Cotton / Betsey Brockway / Samuel Rackleff identification. Full census original archived in Library and analyzed in `JESSIE_1880_ELBRIDGE_ORIGINAL_AND_PARENT_CROSSCHECK_2026-10-11.md`.
3. **29 November 1881 Julius birth**, Elbridge, original p322 entry213, names **John [initial unread] Aurene** and **Amelia Aurene**, father farmer, mother NY-born, father's birthplace explicitly **Unknown**.

**Evidence assessment:** The two adjacent 1879 and 1881 birth returns, coupled to a matching intervening 1880 family census, very strongly identify a stable family unit headed by a man called John/possibly Jonas Aurin[e] and Amelia in Oceana County. However **neither 1881 record nor the others establishes that John/“Jonas” is the same man as Karl Johan Aurén (LZD1-X5W)** on FamilySearch. A separate compiled **Karl Johan Aurén born 1853** married Karolina Hult in 1883; if this were the same man, 1880 father age36 suggests a **~10-year age conflict**. The marriage and true Swedish baptism/age remain unproved. Do not merge on name similarity.

**Notice:** The 1881 father's birthplace being *Unknown* is a **missing datum**, not evidence he was born outside Sweden. Do not overwrite the 1879/1880 Swedish statements to unknown.
