# Atlas 2D portrait crop review register — November 23, 2026

## Implemented result
The 2D Tree reads **25 provenance-linked portrait entries**: seven existing individual portraits plus **18 new cropped views** of preserved original photographs. Six new views identify a person within a captioned two-person or group photograph; twelve isolate a source-labeled individual portrait.

Crop coordinates in `atlas-2d-portrait-index-20261123.js` are fractional `[left,top,width,height]` relative to the original image. The Tree canvas applies these coordinates *when drawing*; there is no image invention, facial recognition, or overwrite. Every new portrait image is already packaged as a repository asset. The original remains accessible via the photo source.

**Attribution is not proof of ancestry.** The source class refers to who a picture depicts, not whether the genealogical relationship is resolved. Contributor-supplied repairs or enhancements are retained as submitted and are not falsely labeled untouched camera negatives.

## Explicit exclusions and caution

- **Isaiah Hill and Lydia Ross photograph:** the repository's `hill-feud-media-and-dunbar-note.js` says the Isaiah pictured with Lydia is *Isaiah and Lucy Murphy Hill's son*, probably Russell's brother, **not the older Isaiah Hill ancestor** on the tree. The registry deliberately excludes `isaiah_hill`, despite the tempting filename. His node retains the neutral silhouette.
- **Five children in Sarah Carling's 1891 photograph:** the approved caption names Clark, Henry, Burr, Owen and Catherine but does not give reliable individual left-to-right positions. We crop *only Sarah*, the seated central adult; we do not invent which child is Jonathan Henry.
- **Family and research-frontier scenes:** no unlabeled face, historical reenactment, synthetic scene, or non-person landscape is assigned as a person portrait.
- When the original image cannot load, the Tree shows the neutral silhouette instead of borrowing a different person's face.

## Display behavior
At normal 2D portrait-card zoom, photos are shown beside names. At wide overview zoom the existing abstract nodes intentionally remain for legibility and performance. The renderer computes an aspect-ratio-preserving source-cover rectangle inside the registered crop and places attribution context in the selected person's existing status line. Tap **Life & records** for the full original person page and photo context.

## Future source audits
The repository contains many more images, but filenames alone are insufficient to identify people. Continue only where the person-page captions, positional descriptions, originals, and external memory provenance establish the particular subject. New crop entries should pass the same bound, existence and identity checks before publication. Some portraits are family-attributed rather than independently proven, and should remain marked as such.
