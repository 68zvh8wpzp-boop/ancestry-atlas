# Ancestry Atlas v3.8.19 biography-distinctness contract

This amendment applies to every existing and future guided biography.

## Narrative ownership

1. Before a biography is approved, compare its complete narration against every
   active biography in the Atlas—not only its immediate parent and child.
2. Each shared event must have one primary biography. That biography owns the
   detailed account, historical setting, emotional consequences and principal
   visuals.
3. Another biography may use one concise bridge sentence when the shared event
   is necessary for continuity. It may not repeat the itinerary, scene-setting,
   hardship sequence, historical explanation or conclusion.
4. Spouses and parent-child pairs must not become alternate tellings of the same
   life. Their biographies should begin from their distinct evidence, actions,
   experience and legacy.
5. When substantial duplication is found, remove at least eighty percent of the
   redundant material. Either biography may become shorter; completeness is not
   measured by equal length.

## Visual ownership

1. A shared event normally has one primary visual sequence in the biography that
   owns it.
2. Do not repeat the same family photograph or archival substitute merely
   because both people were present. A second use requires distinct approved
   narrative work and an explicit exception in the duplication audit.
3. Map-only scenes must render the map in the main slideshow stage. Metadata for
   a small locator does not satisfy a promised map slide.

## Release workflow

1. Run the all-pairs duplication audit before narration approval and again after
   scene allocation.
2. Automated checks flag repeated five-word sequences, highly similar sentences
   and similar paragraphs. A human review decides whether a match is a necessary
   bridge or duplicated storytelling.
3. Record the owner, permitted bridge and required deletion for every flagged
   shared event.
4. Any narration revision makes its prior audio stale immediately. The old audio
   must be disabled until a matching recording is approved and checksummed.
5. A biography cannot enter a release with an unreviewed duplication flag.

## Required collaborative-product behavior

The eventual authoring application must show authors potentially overlapping
biographies while they edit, identify which biography owns each shared event,
and warn when narration or images repeat. Reassignment must automatically mark
the affected narration, scene timing and audio as stale.
