## Distinguishing this man from Jonas Aurin

**Earlier truly examined originals (all archived in this Atlas Library):**
1. **2 February 1879, Crystal, Oceana, MI:** original marriage register vol 2 p39 entry 6, **Jonas/Johas Aurin**, 36, **born Sweden**, farmer, married Amelia [middle initial/surname handwriting disputed]; both resident Crystal.
2. **25 October 1879, Crystal:** original Michigan birth return p276 entry16 for **Jessie Auene**, father **John Auene** (born Sweden), mother **Amelia Auene** (born New York), father farmer.
3. **19 June 1880, Elbridge:** original census with **Jonas Aurin 36**, Swedish-born farmer, wife Amelia L.26 born NY, daughters Iva/Frances/Jessie; next household Samuel Rackliff, Betsey A. and stepdaughter Lillie, consistent with 1886 Cotton family history.
4. **29 November 1881, Elbridge:** original birth p322 entry213 of **Julius S. Aurene**, parents **John Aurene** and **Amelia Aurene**, mother New York, father birthplace entered Unknown, occupation farmer.

**Newly examined Worcester original:** **Charles Auren 29**, son of **Charles and Christine**, marrying **Carolina Hult 29**, daughter of Gustaf and Christina, in 1882, with **1883 Massachusetts Agnes C. Auren** and **1884 Charles Philip Auren** births indexed on Carolina's FamilySearch profile (their original pages remain unread).

**Assessment:** The Worcester groom is **roughly 7–10 years younger** than the Michigan groom, marries a different woman within 3 years, and is supported by an independent later New England household. These together **strongly favor a FamilySearch LZD1-X5W conflation of two Swedish-born name-similar men**, not a proven single man. However this original does **not** specify Charles's previous marriages, and does not directly identify the Michigan husband's later fate; strictly speaking no irrevocable identity determination is possible from the marriage originals alone. Do not automatically detach/merge FamilySearch relationships or propagate either Swedish parental pair to Jessie.

**Comparison with Swedish Carl baptized in Karlskoga in 1849:** separate FamilySearch birth-index FLTG-37N identifies Carl b28 Dec1849, father Carl Auren, mother Stina Maria Ersdr; possible overlap of Charles/Carl father's name, but year would make that child 32 in 1882, not recorded 29, and maternal identity unproved. The 1849 source is indexed only and must not be promoted.
